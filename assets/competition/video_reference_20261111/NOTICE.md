# Original video-guided development models

Six original parameterized meshes and procedural textures, created for this workspace
on 2026-11-11 from visual inspection of user-provided RGB recordings. These are not
official competition meshes, photogrammetry scans, or measured replicas. Existing
provisional bounding dimensions and mass ranges are retained.

Generator: `scripts/prepare_video_fruit.py`. No third-party model or texture was
incorporated. Previous YCB assets and their attribution remain in their own directory.

Detailed provenance, file hashes, and collision limitations: `manifest.json`.
